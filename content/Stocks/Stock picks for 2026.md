From the broader market perspective, there has been three important trends that has been happening:

1. S&P500 is becoming less diversified and more capital is flowing to the top caps. What is originally widely known as the MAG7 doesn't really portray what the market is rewarding right now. The top players & sectors in the markets are known as the "market leaders" and right now they have positive alpha compared to just investing in the S&P500.

> The biggest sectors that are leading this includes memory & semiconductor. 

2. Since the advancement of AI and the rush of companies trying to adopt AI + data centers, there is HUGE need in energy and memory, (not chips). Even though memory (RAM specifically) has been already ordered and readied for the next few years, the demand for it is so massive that the market leader for this sector is obvious: Sandisk (`$SNDK`) followed by Micron Technologies (`$MU`). 

> Earlier in the year, I wanted to allocate capital into these two stocks specifically because of the obvious demands these market leaders in the semiconductor/tech space want. And in fact it was the top play for 2025 & 2026.

3. Because there is a huge demand for energy, leaders in the energy sector like Exxon Mobile (`$XOM`) also have benefited massively. But that does not mean there is enough energy to supply to all these new AI data centers being built. That is why I wanted to look at a stock with the ticker symbol `$SMR` which is Nuscale Power Corp. When it comes to energy, nuclear energy is a great and efficient way to supply the energy needed for these new data centers. However, massive nuclear facilities are NOT approximate and takes a long time to build. That is why there are smaller nuclear reactors that can do the same job and cheaper than traditional energy source alternatives. I think already 10-20 deals has been made by `$SMR` with big tech companies, which means that there is very high upside for this stock in the next few weeks or even coming months. Definitely a stock I will slowly accumulate and respond back in a later note to see if the stock was in fact a correct investment.

4. In the semiconductor space, the biggest winners that are likely going to continue higher will be the trinity INTEL, AMD, NVIDIA. A year or two ago, when there was the debate whether AMD or NVIDIA was going to win out in the war of AI + chips, the obvious frontrunner was NVDA. However, when there is so much demand for the first option, the second best option AMD is probably likely a good play as well. And in fact, going with any of these picks would be the correct choice. Timing doesn't matter, exposure would bring the most benefit. The story behind INTEL was that it was also an obvious winner for 2025-2026 because of Trump(US)'s investment of 8 billion dollars. Now all three of them are going to all time highs again after the reverse of the Iran war dump.
